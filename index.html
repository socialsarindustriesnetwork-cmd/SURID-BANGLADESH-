Who We Are

Working with communities for lasting change.

SURID Bangladesh is a non-governmental, non-profit and non-political development organization committed to inclusive, participatory and environmentally responsible programmes.

Our approach combines community participation, gender equality, safeguarding, transparent governance and evidence-based learning.
